# Design Direction: Kang CV Mu

Reading this as: Modern Productivity Platform with Frosted Glassmorphism for Indonesian job seekers, dial ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1.

## Dials
- **ENERGY: 2 (Balanced)**. Professional, welcoming landing page with subtle frosted glass surfaces, followed by an efficient, distraction-free ATS resume builder.
- **RHYTHM: 2 (Balanced)**. Clear page separation: Hero -> Fitur Utama -> Profil & Kontak Pembuat, seamlessly transitioning to the Split-Screen CV Workspace.
- **MOTION: 1 (Minimal)**. Crisp view transitions, responsive hover states, zero sluggish or distracting animations.

## Visual Identity & Palette (Glassmorphism & Contrast)
- **Background Canvas**: Soft slate gradient mesh (`radial-gradient(at 10% 10%, #e0e7ff 0%, transparent 40%), radial-gradient(at 90% 90%, #dbeafe 0%, transparent 50%), #f1f5f9`)
- **Frosted Glass Cards**:
  - Background: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.78)`
  - Backdrop filter: `blur(16px)`
  - Border: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.65)`
  - Shadow: `0 10px 30px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.07), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`
- **Text & Contrast (WCAG AA Compliant)**:
  - Heading & Primary text: `#0F172A` (Slate 900, Contrast ratio 14:1+)
  - Body & Subtitle text: `#334155` (Slate 700, Contrast ratio 7.5:1+)
  - Accent Primary Button: `#2563EB` with `#FFFFFF` text (Contrast ratio 4.6:1)
  - Secondary / Dark Button: `#0F172A` with `#FFFFFF` text (Contrast ratio 16:1)
- **ATS Resume Paper**: Pure white `#FFFFFF` with strict single-column classical typography (`Times New Roman` or `Calibri`) to ensure 100% compliance with ATS parsers and zero visual artifacts upon printing.
